ふりがな整備士 (Furigana Seibishi) is an iPhone and iPad app for foreign students and mechanics in Japan preparing for the national Automotive Maintenance Skill Registration Examination.

Four tools for every question

  1. Furigana

    On every kanji in questions, choices and explanations. Turn it off with one button to practice in exam conditions.

  2. Reference translations in 7 languages

    For questions and explanations: English, Nepali, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Sinhala and Burmese. Translations are AI-generated references; if the meaning differs, the Japanese text is correct.

    Sinhala and Burmese cover only part of the grade 2 body and electrical questions.

  3. An explanation for every choice

    See why each choice is right or wrong, one by one.

  4. Technical terms in your language

    Look up the automotive terms used in each question, plus a list of basic words that often appear in the exam.

Built from experience running exam-prep classes at a mechanic school.

How you can practice

  • Pass Pack — a set of frequently tested questions, chosen from past exam trends and the new comprehensive textbooks: grade 3 500, grade 2 600, grade 1 450 questions. It gives you 20 questions at a time from the ones you have not mastered yet.
  • Full past exams — the same number of questions as the real exam, with remaining time and a pass/fail result.
  • Practice questions for the new fields — the comprehensive exam from March 2027 adds new fields such as electronic control systems. Where past questions do not cover them, the app adds original practice questions written for the scope of the new comprehensive exam. We will keep adding more. When you buy a grade, new questions for that grade are included at no extra cost. (Practice questions are not real exam questions and are labeled as such.)
  • By field, retry your mistakes and favorites, and scores by field.
  • Offline, no account — works without internet, no sign-up, and no personal data is collected.

What is included

Past questions of the written Automotive Maintenance Skill Registration Examination, as published by JASPA (Japan Automobile Service Promotion Association): grades 2 and 3 from October 2016 to October 2026, and grade 1 (small vehicles) from March 2017 to March 2026.

From 14 March 2027, the grade 2 and grade 3 written exams change to a single “comprehensive” (総合) exam; grade 1 changes from March 2028 at the earliest. There are no past comprehensive exam questions yet. The app re-organizes earlier past questions into the comprehensive exam's fields (engine, chassis, electronic control, equipment and basics, regulations).

Questions, choices and answers were checked against the original question papers and answer sheets. In October 2026 the developer asked JASPA in writing about offering the questions with furigana, translations and explanations in an app, and were told that permission is not required.

This app is made by an individual. It is not endorsed or supervised by JASPA.

Questions

Is the exam in English?

No. The automotive mechanic exam in Japan is in Japanese only. This app keeps every question in the original Japanese and adds furigana and reference translations to help you understand it. You can turn furigana off to practice in exam conditions.

When does the exam change to the comprehensive (総合) format?

For grades 2 and 3, from 14 March 2027. For grade 1 (small vehicles), March 2028 at the earliest. There are no past comprehensive exam questions yet; the app re-organizes earlier past questions into the comprehensive exam's fields.

Is it free?

One full comprehensive practice set per grade (grade 2: 40 questions, grade 3: 30, grade 1: 50) is free. All questions for a grade are a one-time purchase: JPY 980 (grade 3), JPY 1,500 (grade 2), JPY 1,800 (grade 1). No subscription.

Are the translations correct?

Translations and explanations are AI-generated references. If the meaning differs, the Japanese question is correct.

Is this an official JASPA app?

No. It is made by an individual and is not endorsed or supervised by JASPA (Japan Automobile Service Promotion Association). The questions are the past exam questions that JASPA publishes on its website. In October 2026 the developer asked JASPA in writing about offering them with furigana, translations and explanations in an app, and were told that permission is not required.

Will this app make me pass?

No app can promise that. It is a tool for practicing past questions.
