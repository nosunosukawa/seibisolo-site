Past questions for the written exam, in the original Japanese, with furigana on every kanji and reference translations in 7 languages. For iPhone and iPad.

A single “comprehensive” (総合) written exam for grades 2 and 3 starts on 14 March 2027. Applications: 14 to 18 December 2026, at your local Automobile Service Promotion Association (自動車整備振興会). Please check the official notice.

What it does

  • Furigana on every kanji. You can switch it off.
  • AI-made reference translations: English, Nepali, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Sinhala and Burmese.
  • An explanation of why each choice is right or wrong.
  • After purchase: past questions by sitting and by field, a “Pass Pack” (it does not predict the exam), and review of the ones you got wrong.
  • Works offline. No account. No personal data collected.

What it does not do

  • It does not guarantee that you will pass.
  • It is not on Android yet (in preparation, no date).
  • It has no practical or oral exam practice.
  • There are no past papers of the comprehensive exam yet. Original practice questions are labeled as not real exam questions.
  • Translations may contain mistakes. The Japanese text is always the correct one. Burmese and Sinhala are missing or partial for grade 2 body and electrical questions.

About 4 in 10 past questions ask for the incorrect one: 「不適切なものはどれか」. See the 30 most common words in exam questions.

How it is made

Questions, choices and answers are checked against the question papers and answer sheets. Explanations and translations are made with AI. The app is not endorsed or supervised by JASPA (日整連). In October 2026 the developer asked JASPA in writing about offering the published questions with furigana, translations and explanations in an app, and JASPA replied that permission is not required.
