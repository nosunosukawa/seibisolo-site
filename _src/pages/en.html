Furigana Seibishi is an iPhone and iPad app of past questions from the written part of the exam (Automotive Maintenance Skill Registration Examination; grade 1 small vehicles, grade 2, grade 3). It is made by one person, a teacher who has run exam-prep classes at a mechanic school.

A single “comprehensive” (総合) written exam for grades 2 and 3 starts on 14 March 2027. Some earlier exam types are still listed for that sitting. Applications for that sitting are accepted from 14 to 18 December 2026 at your local Automobile Service Promotion Association (自動車整備振興会). Please check the official notice.

What it does

  • The questions stay in the original Japanese, because the exam is in Japanese. Furigana can be shown on every kanji, and switched off.
  • AI-made reference translations of questions and explanations: English, Nepali, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Sinhala and Burmese.
  • An explanation of why each choice is right or wrong.
  • After purchase: past questions by sitting (with a timer and an estimated pass/fail), by field, a “Pass Pack” selected from past questions (it does not predict the exam), and retrying the ones you got wrong.
  • Works offline. No account. No personal data collected.

What it does not do

  • It does not guarantee that you will pass.
  • It is not on Android yet (in preparation, no date). The 5-question trial works in any browser.
  • It has no practical or oral exam practice.
  • There are no past papers of the comprehensive exam, because it has not been held yet. The app re-organizes earlier past questions into the new fields and adds original practice questions, which are labeled as not real exam questions.
  • It is not endorsed or supervised by JASPA (日整連).
  • Translations may contain mistakes. The Japanese text is always the correct one. Burmese and Sinhala are missing or partial for grade 2 body and electrical questions.

A tip: read the end of the question

About 4 in 10 past questions (2,144 of 5,371 in the app) ask for the incorrect one: 「不適切なものはどれか」. See the 30 most common words in exam questions, with translations, free.

How it is made

Questions, choices and answers are checked against the question papers and answer sheets. Explanations and translations are made with AI and are still being reviewed. In October 2026 the developer asked JASPA in writing about offering the published questions with furigana, translations and explanations in an app; JASPA replied that permission is not required.
